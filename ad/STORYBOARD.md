# The film — storyboard

16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, ~50 s. Black, white SF Pro type, slow light, hard cuts, one music bed, no voice-over.
Generated shots come from Higgsfield (Veo 3.1 / Nano Banana Pro); the phone, the map and the end card are
real HTML rendered frame by frame. Final: `ad/roadster.mp4`. Poster: `ad/poster.png`.
